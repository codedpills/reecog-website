FAQ

Quick answers about Reecog, the Slack recognition bot for employee appreciation and values-based recognition.

How long is the free trial?

30 days. No credit card required to start.

Is pricing per user or per workspace?

Per workspace. Pick the tier that matches your team size.

Can I customize recognition values and rewards?

Yes. Admins can manage values, daily limits, rewards, and GIF settings from the app home.

Do you support multi-user recognition?

Yes. Recognize multiple teammates or user groups in one message.

What kind of GIFs do you use?

Only PG-rated GIFs. You can also disable GIFs if you want.

Where is my data stored?

We use secure storage and only keep what is needed for recognition and rewards. More details are in the Privacy page.

Is Reecog good for work from home Slack teams?

Yes. Reecog helps distributed teams keep Slack communication social and fun, while reinforcing company values.
