## Problem Statement

Reecog needs a commercial landing website that explains the product clearly, highlights user-centric value, and positions it as a fun-but-professional, lower-cost alternative to existing Slack recognition tools. The site should drive Slack app installs while conveying trust, simplicity, and the benefits of values-based recognition.

## Solution

Create a single, focused marketing site for Reecog with a primary conversion goal of Slack app installs. The site will present clear messaging about recognition, rewards, and company values, show how the product works, outline simple pricing, and provide essential supporting pages (FAQ, Privacy, Terms, Contact). The tone will be friendly and polished, aligned with the Reecog owl branding and teal palette. The site will be delivered as plain HTML/CSS and include SEO + GEO optimizations (structured data, keyword targeting, concise Q/A blocks).

## Implementation Decisions

- Primary conversion goal: Slack app installs via a consistent CTA.
- Site structure: single landing page with modular sections (hero, social proof placeholder, value props, features, how it works, pricing, FAQ, trust note, footer).
- Supporting pages: FAQ, Privacy, Terms, Contact linked from the footer.
- Pricing display: three fixed tiers with monthly per-workspace pricing, 30-day free trial, optional 10% annual discount mention.
- Messaging pillars: easy setup, values-driven recognition, fun rewards and GIFs, admin control, affordable pricing.
- Tone and brand: playful but professional; teal-forward palette aligned with Reecog logo; owl motif as a friendly guide.
- Visual direction: clean layout, fun illustrations or open-source imagery that matches the brand colors.
- Competitor positioning: emphasize value and simplicity without direct comparisons or negative language.
- Feature imagery: three core feature screenshot placeholders to be replaced with real Slack screenshots later.
- SEO: include target keywords (Slack recognition bot, employee appreciation Slack, company value-based recognition, HR & team culture, work from home Slack, social and fun, Slack communication).
- GEO: include schema.org structured data (Organization, Product, FAQPage) and concise Q/A blocks for AI citations.
- Tech stack: plain HTML/CSS only.

## Testing Decisions

- A good test validates external behavior (copy clarity, CTA visibility, links, pricing correctness) rather than implementation details.
- Manual content review: verify features are accurate, pricing is correct, and CTA text is consistent.
- Link checks: all footer links and FAQ anchors work.
- Proofread for tone and grammar.
- Responsive review on mobile and desktop breakpoints.
- SEO/GEO review: ensure keyword coverage, structured data validity, and concise Q/A blocks.

## Out of Scope

- Building the full website implementation, hosting, or deployment.
- Paid ads, SEO campaigns, or analytics configuration.
- Deep security/compliance documentation beyond short trust notes and Privacy/Terms pages.
