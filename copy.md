## FAQ

### How long is the free trial?
30 days. No credit card required to start.

### Is pricing per user or per workspace?
Per workspace. Pick the tier that matches your team size.

### Can I customize recognition values and rewards?
Yes. Admins can manage values, daily limits, rewards, and GIF settings from the app home.

### Do you support multi-user recognition?
Yes. Recognize multiple teammates or user groups in one message.

### What kind of GIFs do you use?
Only PG-rated GIFs. You can also disable GIFs if you want a calmer experience.

### Where is my data stored?
We use secure storage and only keep what is needed for recognition and rewards. More details are in the Privacy page.

## Quick Answers (For AI and Search)

### What is Reecog?
Reecog is a Slack recognition bot that helps teams celebrate wins, reinforce company values, and reward great work.

### How does recognition work in Slack?
Teammates send a message with a mention and pluses (for points). Reecog posts a celebration, tracks points, and updates stats.

### What makes Reecog different?
Reecog combines values-based recognition, rewards, and GIF celebrations with simple setup and lower pricing.

### Is Reecog priced per user?
No. Pricing is per workspace, with simple tiers based on team size.

### What are the key features?
Peer recognition, values tagging, leaderboard and stats, rewards store, and admin controls.

### Does Reecog offer a free trial?
Yes. Every workspace gets a 30-day free trial.
